About FitSquad Hub

FitSquad Hub is a community fitness platform where members can join workout challenges, track their progress, and stay motivated with their squad.

Members can complete fitness quests, track workout duration, and earn XP reward points. Challenges are available at different difficulty levels, from Beginner to Advanced.

FitSquad Hub makes fitness more fun, social, and motivating while helping members reach their fitness goals.
