Welcome to the FitSquad Hub!

FitSquad Hub is a community fitness challenge platform where squad members join quests, log their workouts, and keep each other accountable. Every challenge tracks its duration and awards XP reward points, so your progress is always visible and every finished workout feels like a win.

Whether you are lacing up for your first 5K or grinding through an advanced strength program, there is a quest built for your level, and a squad cheering you on along the way.
