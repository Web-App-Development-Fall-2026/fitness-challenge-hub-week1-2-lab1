Welcome to FitSquad Hub!

FitSquad Hub is a community-driven fitness platform where members take on shared workout quests together. Every challenge you join tracks your duration and rewards you with XP points as you progress, keeping motivation high and consistency rewarded.

Challenges span every skill level — from Beginner mobility routines to Advanced strength and endurance tiers — so no matter where you're starting from, there's a squad and a quest built for you.
