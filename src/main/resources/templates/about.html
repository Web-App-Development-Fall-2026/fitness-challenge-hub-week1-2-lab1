FitSquad Hub

FitSquad Hub is a community fitness platform that helps people stay active and motivated through fun workout challenges.

Members can track their workout duration, complete fitness quests, and earn XP reward points while working toward their goals. Challenges are available at different difficulty levels, from Beginner to Advanced.
