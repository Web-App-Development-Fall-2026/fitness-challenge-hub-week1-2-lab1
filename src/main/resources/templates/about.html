FitSquad Hub

Tagline

FitSquad Hub is a community fitness platform where squad members join monthly workout quests and keep each other accountable.

Every workout tracks its duration and earns XP reward points, so progress feels like leveling up in a game.

Challenges come in difficulty tiers from Beginner to Advanced, so anyone can join and keep pushing further.

  • Community fitness quests
  • Duration and XP tracking
  • Beginner → Advanced tiers
